1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Stoneward Security public website and to business inquiries sent to us by form or email. A signed service agreement, statement of work, or data-processing agreement may include additional terms for information handled during a client engagement. If those terms conflict with this policy, the signed agreement controls for that engagement.

Important: The public inquiry form is not a secure channel for credentials, authentication codes, API keys, financial or health information, sensitive personal data, or raw security logs.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact details such as your name, work email, phone number, and company.
  • Your selected service and the environment description or message you submit.
  • Correspondence, scheduling information, proposals, contracts, and payment records if we begin a business relationship.
  • Technical information you authorize us to receive for a client engagement, subject to the applicable written scope.

Information collected automatically

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Our form processor may also process IP address, browser, timestamp, and anti-spam information when a form is submitted.

Optional analytics

If you select “Allow analytics,” we load Google Analytics to measure page visits, general device and browser information, approximate location derived from IP address, referral source, CTA clicks, email-link clicks, and successful lead-form submissions. Stoneward configures the tag without Google signals or advertising-personalization signals and does not send form-field contents to Analytics. The Google tag remains unloaded when you decline or have not made a choice.

Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under stoneward_analytics_consent. You may revisit the choice through “Analytics settings” in the footer. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries, schedule calls, and recommend an appropriate service.
  • Prepare proposals, deliver authorized services, and provide client support.
  • Secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and business operations.
  • Detect spam, fraud, abuse, or threats.
  • Maintain business, tax, and legal records and comply with applicable obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use inquiry data for targeted advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, a client agreement, dispute resolution, security, or applicable recordkeeping requirements.

  • Public form submissions: Web3Forms provides 30 days of submission history on its free plan and states that submission data may be retained for up to three years under its maximum retention policy. We do not control provider backup or security-log schedules.
  • Email and business records: Retained while an inquiry or relationship is active and afterward only as reasonably needed for business, legal, tax, or security purposes.
  • Client technical data: Handled under the written engagement scope and our Data Handling Policy.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask us to access, correct, delete, or provide a copy of personal information associated with you. You may also object to or request limits on certain processing. Rights vary by location and may be subject to identity verification, legal exceptions, and recordkeeping requirements.

Send requests to stonewardsecurity@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request.” We will respond as required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for making a valid privacy request.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No transmission, system, or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects information for which Stoneward is responsible, we will investigate and provide notices as required by applicable law and contract.

8. Children

Our website and services are designed for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, providers, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date. Material changes may receive additional notice when appropriate.
