FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

What does ongoing monitoring mean?

Scheduled automated runs (weekly or monthly) that scan live threat intelligence and your latest telemetry, with alerts surfaced in your monthly report. It's monitoring and analysis on your schedule — not a 24/7 SOC, and not priced like one.

Is this a guarantee of security?

No — and we say so in writing. Reports are advisory, human-reviewed, and clearly disclaimed. No service can honestly guarantee security; we make that explicit so there are no surprises.

What happens to our logs?

Logs are analyzed in temporary storage and deleted at the end of each run — or, with Tier 2 client-site analysis, they never leave your network at all. Only the report — with short evidence snippets (max 180 characters) — is retained, per our Data Handling Policy.

Can you analyze our logs without them leaving our network?

Yes — Tier 2 client-site analysis. We send a small, code-signed collector that runs on your premises (Windows, macOS, or Linux; no installs needed). It analyzes offline, redacts secrets automatically, hashes IPs on request, and produces a findings file with a SHA-256 checksum you verify out-of-band. Raw logs never leave your network — that's contractual, in our DPA.

Do we need any technical skills?

No. If you can describe your systems on a 30-minute call, we handle the analysis and deliver a plain-language report with a prioritized action plan. For monitoring, we can connect Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace / firewall telemetry for you.
